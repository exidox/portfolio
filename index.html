I'm a computer science graduate specializing in AI and Human–Computer Interaction , with a focus on building systems that are not just intelligent, but meaningful to people. My work explores the intersection of machine learning, design, and human cognition.

Currently completing MSc HCI at Paris-Saclay with a research internship at INRIA Ex-Situ (supervised by Wendy Mackay). Previously: BTech Computer Science with specialisation in AI/ML.

I believe the most interesting computing happens not on screens but in tanglible interfaces that respond and morph to human input and the environment
